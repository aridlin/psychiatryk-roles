Consultant world

psychiatryk_roles:konsultanci is a separate overworld-style dimension generated from vanilla biomes and terrain.

Consultants remain in Survival mode. Inside this dimension they can build, fight, use containers and entities, mount vehicles, pick up items, and drop items normally. The staff returns them to their saved position in the original dimension.

The dimension uses server-synchronized vanilla data and adds no client-side mod requirement.

Dynmap detects it as psychiatryk_roles_konsultanci and exposes three map layers.

Language and maintenance

/konsultant status [player] shows role, world mode, language, permission conditions, and expiry timers.

/konsultant-log [page] and /konsultant-log player <name> [page] show the persistent audit log in game.

/konsultant-item preset list lists ready-made grants. Use preset give <player> <preset> or preset give-timed <player> <preset> <seconds>.

/polski and /english save the player's language, refresh their consultant item display, and replay the consultant explainer every time.

Language selection gives a localized handbook. Hostile mobs in the protected world retaliate only against the consultant who provoked them, for 30 seconds.

/konsultant-item clear <name> removes marked consultant items from an online or offline player's inventory and Ender Chest.

Ordinary items can always be dropped. A consultant can pick their own dropped stack back up; protected consultant equipment remains undroppable in the restricted world.

The server JAR is loaded only on restart. This page may describe a staged build before the running process has restarted.

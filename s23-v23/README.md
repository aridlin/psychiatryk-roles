# S23 v23

E opens the normal inventory with large Settings and Help buttons and Storage/Music access. Dedicated-server client class loading is fixed. Saddle smithing adds an optional passenger; bound vehicles keep owner-only drivers. Frost Walker supports lava; Breach breaks protected, thin walls above 60 km/h with hot-reloaded cooldown. Knockback trips mobs and players; Piercing scales with speed. Fresh rentals start at 20-49% battery, show a dial and no-parking warning, charge 100 chips and 5 damage on forbidden dismount, and sink/pulverize in snow blocks or snow layers over snow blocks. Recall defaults to R, rebindable in Controls, and retains owner/Loyalty checks.

- Netherite block smithing upgrade: normal acceleration below the standard top speed, then climbs from 67 to 180 km/h over about two minutes, gentle descent, stronger air steering and 3-block steps above 100 km/h.
- Right-click a firework while riding: 20-second nitro, 200 km/h cap, attached rockets and trail. Hold Space for upward thrust. Creative does not consume the rocket.
- Lime and Bolt rental liveries; the regular KuKirin textures are unchanged.
- Rentals knock the real player prone with a tumble and timed recovery, protected by a cooldown. This is an animated knockdown, not a joint-based rigid-body ragdoll.

Runtime evidence and exact activation/rollback checks are included. The final GUI-only changes were compared against the complete passing candidate. Motor hum was not retested at the user's request. User-supplied server WAVs and credentials are excluded.

Riding demo: https://bin.aridlin.pl/file/0/scooter-demo-upload.mp4 (temporary bin upload).
Natural-road mods remain under evaluation; none added to this release.

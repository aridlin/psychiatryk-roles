# Peeb and music — prepared 3.0.5 player guide

These controls describe the isolated 3.0.5 candidate. They do not mean the
production server or download pack has already switched to 3.0.5.

## Peeb

Craft one **string + leather + stick**. Hold Peeb to transform, or Shift +
right-click to wear it in the chest slot. Remove helmet, leggings and boots
first. Peeb supplies seven natural armour and prevents fall damage while active.

Hold **left-click** while the target circle is on a reachable collision surface
to grapple; release to let go. The default maximum range is eight blocks.
The hook pulls immediately, reels quickly and preserves sideways swing momentum.
The camera aims from slightly above Peeb’s head. Right-click keeps
normal interactions and **E** opens inventory, including while Peeb is worn.

Freelook prefers **Left Alt**; Better MC may already use it, in which case Peeb
chooses another unused key. Read the actual key shown in the Peeb HUD or check
Options → Controls → Peeb Adventures. Unassigned optional bindings stay unbound
if all candidates conflict. `/peebcamera 2..10` changes the camera distance.
`/scooteradmin` → Peeb opens the operator's configurable Peeb settings.

## Music

Use scooter Music, Shift + right-click a placed jukebox, or `/jukebox` for an
item in the back slot. Favorite songs, enable next-track autoplay or shuffle,
and choose Loop for repeating playback. Operator settings can choose Master or
Music category. `/musichud` controls the song overlay and persistent HUD.

The YouTube/YT Music import screen accepts supported public song/playlist URLs
and reports the server import result. Imported tracks appear in the shared song
list. Metadata/cover art may arrive after audio starts; the overlay updates when
it becomes available. Nearby players hear positional sources with Sound Physics.
Unequipping a worn jukebox or stopping playback ends that source.

Custom songs on a placed jukebox display a vinyl spinning at 33⅓ RPM. Tracks
without a cover still show the vinyl and rotating label; cover art replaces the
label when it arrives. Stopping playback or breaking the jukebox removes the
record. This is included in the separate prepared jukebox-disc follow-up.

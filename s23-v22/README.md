# S23 v22

**Tested and staged, not deployed.** The October 4 restart vote expired without approval; production and public download pointers remain v21.

Scooter storage has Settings and searchable Music buttons. Both return to storage and fit Minecraft GUI scale. Recall defaults to R, rebindable in Controls, and retains owner/Loyalty checks.

- Netherite block smithing upgrade: normal acceleration below the standard top speed, then 180 km/h, gentle descent, stronger air steering and 3-block steps above 100 km/h.
- Right-click a firework while riding: 20-second nitro, 200 km/h cap, attached rockets and trail. Hold Space for upward thrust. Creative does not consume the rocket.
- Lime and Bolt rental liveries; the regular KuKirin textures are unchanged.
- Rentals knock the real player prone with a tumble and timed recovery, protected by a cooldown. This is an animated knockdown, not a joint-based rigid-body ragdoll.

Runtime evidence and exact activation/rollback checks are included. The final GUI-only changes were compared against the complete passing candidate. Motor hum was not retested at the user's request. User-supplied server WAVs and credentials are excluded.
